Frequently asked questions

Who can see my request?

Only the Prayer Department. A request is never shown publicly unless you agree to the Prayer Board, and then only without your name and after it has been reviewed.

Can I send a request anonymously?

Yes. Your name is hidden from the team's list. For safety, the email of the Google account you signed in with is still visible to the Prayer Department.

Why do I need to sign in with Google?

It keeps the form safe from spam and lets you see the status of your own requests later.

Can I delete or change my request?

You can delete a request, withdraw it from the Prayer Board, or mark it as answered anytime from "My requests" after signing in.

What does "urgent" mean?

Use it when you would like the team to pray as soon as possible. It is not for emergencies. If someone is in danger, call 911, or call or text 988 in the US.

What is the Prayer Board?

A public page where reviewed requests are shown without names, so others can pray along. Joining is always optional.

Privacy & data

What we collect

  • Your name (unless you choose anonymous), grade, topic and the request you write.
  • The email of the Google account you sign in with, even when you send anonymously.

Who can see it

Only the Prayer Department. Your request is never shown publicly unless you tick the Prayer Board consent box, and then only without your name and after it has been reviewed.

Your choices

  • Withdraw Prayer Board consent anytime from "My requests".
  • Delete a request you sent anytime from "My requests".

Safety

If someone may be in danger, the Prayer Department may contact a responsible adult. In an emergency call 911, or call or text 988 in the US.

Community guidelines

  • Write with kindness. Requests are prayed for, not judged.
  • Do not include private details about other people (full names, phone numbers, addresses) unless they have agreed.
  • Keep requests free of insults, bullying or inappropriate content. The Prayer Department may hide or remove them.
  • Requests shown on the Prayer Board are reviewed first and never show your name.
  • This form is not for emergencies. Call 911, or call or text 988 in the US.
