Privacy & data

What we collect

  • Your name (unless you choose anonymous), grade, topic and the request you write.
  • The email of the Google account you sign in with, even when you send anonymously.

Who can see it

Only the Prayer Department. Your request is never shown publicly unless you tick the Prayer Board consent box, and then only without your name and after it has been reviewed.

Your choices

  • Withdraw Prayer Board consent anytime from "My requests".
  • Delete a request you sent anytime from "My requests".

Safety

If someone may be in danger, the Prayer Department may contact a responsible adult. In an emergency call 911, or call or text 988 in the US.
